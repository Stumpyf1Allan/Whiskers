Whiskers - read me first
========================

Whiskers keeps track of your investments, the limits you set for them, and what markets are
doing. It only ever reads from your accounts: it cannot buy, sell or move money. Nothing in it
is financial advice.

1. Open it
   Windows: double-click Whiskers.exe. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your
   PC" because the app isn't signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.
   Mac: unzip, drag Whiskers into Applications, then right-click it and choose Open. If the Mac
   still refuses, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

2. Look around
   A short tour starts the first time. To see every screen working before setting anything
   up, use the sample portfolio (made up); it comes out again in one click. Remove it before
   connecting your own accounts.

3. Set it up
   - Your plan: in Settings, choose "Build a plan from questions", answer ten or so questions,
     and adjust what it suggests. Or load a plan file someone made for you.
   - Trading 212: connect with a read-only API key (the app shows you how).
   - Freetrade: export your activity file from the Freetrade app and import it.
   - Cash accounts and credit cards: add them in Settings and type the balances in.

4. Your data
   Everything stays on your own computer, in Whiskers' own data folder (Settings has a button
   that opens it). Updating or deleting the app never touches it.

5. Updates
   Whiskers tells you when a new version is ready and downloads it for you.
